STANCOFF VERSION 7.5.7

ONE-PAGE PAYMENT RECEIPT

Changes:
- Payment receipt optimized for A4 portrait printing.

- Signature section kept together on the same page.

- All existing payment information is retained.
- Receipt is designed to fit on one printed A4 page in normal cases.

DATABASE SAFETY
- No PostgreSQL tables or records are changed.
- Existing payments, attendance, advances and receipts remain intact.
