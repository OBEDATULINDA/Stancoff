STANCOFF UPDATE 6.8.6 - RECEIVE EARLIER STATION TRANSFERS

This update adds a Receive button to single-batch station transfers that were saved before the transfer receiving module was introduced.

Important inventory rule:
Earlier transfers already added the dispatched weight to the receiving location. The receipt therefore adjusts destination inventory only by the difference between received and dispatched weight. It does not add the received weight a second time.

Example:
Sent 1,000 kg; received 990 kg -> destination inventory reduces by 10 kg.
Sent 1,000 kg; received 1,005 kg -> destination inventory increases by 5 kg.

Existing PostgreSQL records are preserved.
