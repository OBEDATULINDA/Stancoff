STANCOFF VERSION 7.0 – WORKFORCE & PAYROLL

Added:
- Daily, Weekly and Monthly pay frequencies
- Editable worker pay frequency
- Pay rate history by pay type
- Automatic payroll preview based on worker pay frequency
- Fixed weekly and monthly pay with attendance shown for review
- Duplicate overlapping payment protection
- Updated workforce payment receipt

Existing workers and rates are automatically treated as Daily until changed.
No existing PostgreSQL records are deleted.
