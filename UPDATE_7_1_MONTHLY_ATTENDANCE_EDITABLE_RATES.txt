STANCOFF VERSION 7.1

CHANGES
1. Weekly attendance cards replaced with monthly attendance cards.
2. Each worker/month card shows a calendar with full-day (1), half-day (1/2),
   paid/unpaid status, equivalent days, attendance value, and unpaid attendance.
3. Rate History now has an Edit button.
4. Rate-history edits are audit-logged.
5. Editing a rate-history row does not rewrite the rate/amount already saved
   on older attendance or completed payment records.

DATABASE SAFETY
No tables are dropped or recreated. Existing PostgreSQL data remains in place.

DEPLOYMENT
Upload the contents of Stancoff-main to the existing GitHub repository and
allow Render to redeploy. Do not delete PostgreSQL or change DATABASE_URL.
