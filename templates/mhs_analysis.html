2. Parchment outturn

Weigh the sample, hull it, take moisture, then enter General Outturn and Net Outturn. Defects are calculated automatically as General OT − Net OT.

CalculationDefects % = General Outturn % − Net Outturn %. You do not need to enter hulled sample weight or individual defect weights for parchment.
