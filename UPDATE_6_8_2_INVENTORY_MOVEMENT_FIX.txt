STANCOFF UPDATE 6.8.2 — INVENTORY MOVEMENT FIX

Fixes an error when moving coffee through Inventory, including Drying Yard -> Temporary Storage/Warehouse.

Problem fixed:
- Normal inventory movement incorrectly referenced dry_weight and wet_weight variables.
- Those variables belong only to the Finish Drying workflow.

Correct behaviour:
- Inventory movements use the movement weight entered by the user.
- Source stock decreases by that movement weight.
- Destination stock increases by that movement weight.
- Finish Drying continues to separately use wet input weight and actual dry output weight.

No database tables are deleted or recreated. Existing PostgreSQL records remain intact.
