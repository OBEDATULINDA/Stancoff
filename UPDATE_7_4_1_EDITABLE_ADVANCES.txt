STANCOFF VERSION 7.4.1

Salary Advance History now has an Edit button.

Editable fields:
- Worker
- Pay-cycle start
- Pay-cycle end
- Advance date
- Advance amount
- Notes

Safety:
An advance that has already been deducted from a completed salary payment
cannot be edited directly. This protects the payment and audit trail from
becoming inconsistent.

No existing database records or tables are deleted.
