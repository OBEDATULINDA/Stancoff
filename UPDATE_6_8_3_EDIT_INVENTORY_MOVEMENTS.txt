STANCOFF UPDATE 6.8.3 - EDIT STORAGE AND INVENTORY MOVEMENTS

Added:
- Edit button in Movement History.
- Edit movement date, moisture, responsible person and notes.
- Edit destination and weight when the coffee has not moved onward or been dispatched.
- Automatic correction of source and destination inventory balances.
- Protection for Finished Drying and linked Station Transfer movements.
- Audit log entry whenever a movement is edited.

Existing PostgreSQL tables and records are not deleted or recreated.
