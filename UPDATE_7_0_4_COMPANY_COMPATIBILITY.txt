STANCOFF UPDATE 7.0.4 - COMPANY SCHEMA COMPATIBILITY

Purpose
-------
Fix Mothers Harvest company creation against the existing production PostgreSQL company table.

Change
------
The company seed INSERT explicitly supplies both required legacy columns:
- slug
- is_legacy_stancoff

Mothers Harvest values:
- code: MHS
- slug: mothers-harvest
- is_legacy_stancoff: false

Stancoff values:
- code: STANCOFF
- slug: stancoff
- is_legacy_stancoff: true

Safety
------
No table is dropped or recreated. No existing coffee, supplier, batch, payroll, inventory, sales, station, user, or password data is deleted.
