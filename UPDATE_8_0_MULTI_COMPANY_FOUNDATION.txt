STANCOFF / BUSINESS ERP VERSION 8.0
MULTI-COMPANY FOUNDATION

WHAT THIS RELEASE DOES
- Converts the application shell into a multi-company ERP foundation.
- Preserves Stancoff Company Limited as the protected legacy company.
- Adds a company selector after login.
- Adds a separate dashboard for every non-Stancoff company.
- Adds System Administrator company management.
- Adds per-company user access and per-company roles.
- Adds per-company module configuration.
- Supports custom module placeholders for businesses with different operations.
- Adds a Switch Company option in the sidebar.
- Makes the header/sidebar show the selected company's name and business type.

STANCOFF SAFETY
- All existing Stancoff users are automatically assigned to Stancoff using
  their existing roles.
- The oldest existing Admin is automatically made the first System Administrator.
- Existing Stancoff coffee/workforce/report modules remain enabled.
- Existing operational Stancoff routes are BLOCKED while another company is
  selected, preventing a new company from seeing Stancoff's operational data.
- No existing suppliers, purchases, batches, processing, drying, inventory,
  movements, workforce, payments, advances or other Stancoff operational records
  are deleted or recreated.

IMPORTANT ARCHITECTURE
- Version 8.0 is the company/core layer.
- New companies DO NOT reuse Stancoff operational tables.
- Each new company's modules should now be designed separately based on that
  company's real workflow.
- As individual modules are built, their business tables should be company-scoped.
- Legacy Stancoff operational tables remain dedicated to Stancoff until each
  relevant module is migrated deliberately.

DATABASE CHANGES
- New additive tables:
  company
  company_user
  company_module
  system_admin
- Additive column:
  audit_log.company_id
- Existing audit records are assigned to Stancoff because they predate the
  multi-company system.
- No PostgreSQL database reset is required or permitted.

DEPLOYMENT
- Replace matching repository files with this package.
- Do NOT delete the PostgreSQL database.
- Do NOT change DATABASE_URL.
