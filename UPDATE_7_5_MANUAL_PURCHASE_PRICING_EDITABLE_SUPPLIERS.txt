STANCOFF VERSION 7.5

PURCHASE PRICING
- Purchase entry no longer pulls a single price from Price History.
- Cherry price is entered manually for every supplier delivery.
- Floater price is entered manually when floaters are bought.
- The purchase total is calculated from those actual prices.
- Editing a purchase also allows correction of the actual prices.

PRICE HISTORY
- Every new purchase automatically creates a Price History record.
- Editing a purchase updates its linked Price History record.
- Voiding a purchase marks its linked price record Voided.
- Permanently deleting a purchase deletes its linked automatically-created price row.
- Earlier manual Price History rows remain visible for historical reference.

DASHBOARD
- Adds an all-time weighted average cherry buying price:
  total value of good cherry / total good cherry kilograms.
- This is weighted by kilograms, not a simple average of supplier price entries.

SUPPLIERS
- Supplier entries now have an Edit button.
- Code, name, phone, location and status can be corrected.
- Editing supplier details does not change historical purchase amounts.

DATABASE SAFETY
- Existing purchases, suppliers and price history are preserved.
- Price History receives new nullable traceability columns only.
- No existing tables are dropped or recreated.
