STANCOFF VERSION 8.11.1 — VISIBLE PARTIAL PRODUCTION MODE

- Adds an explicit Full Lot / Partial Production choice to Commercial Coffee Production Entry.
- Full Lot locks source quantities to the complete available balance.
- Partial Production enables Quantity to Process per selected source lot and shows remaining balance.
- Factory reweigh remains separate from selected quantity so input/reweigh variance remains traceable.
- Backend treats Full Lot as authoritative even if a stale browser submits a smaller hidden quantity.
- Applies to the shared Commercial Coffee workspace used by Mothers Harvest and Stancoff Commercial.
- No database reset or new schema change is required beyond Version 8.11.
