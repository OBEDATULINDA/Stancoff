STANCOFF VERSION 7.5.6

ACTIVE WORKERS ONLY
- Monthly attendance/payment cards now display only for workers whose status is Active.
- When a worker is marked Inactive, their monthly cards disappear automatically.
- If the worker is later reactivated, their cards can appear again based on their existing attendance history.

HISTORY PRESERVED
- Inactive workers' attendance history remains in the system.
- Their completed payment records and receipts remain in the system.
- No PostgreSQL records are deleted.

This update changes only what is displayed in the monthly card section.
