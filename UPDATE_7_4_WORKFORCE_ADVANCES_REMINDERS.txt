STANCOFF VERSION 7.4

- Monthly attendance cards follow each worker's own first-attendance date.
- Monthly workers can receive salary advances.
- Advances are normally due two weeks after the worker's rolling monthly cycle starts.
- Matching advances are deducted automatically from monthly salary and cannot be deducted twice.
- Dashboard gives payment reminders when 5 days or fewer remain.
- Dashboard gives monthly advance reminders when 2 days or fewer remain before the advance due date.
- Daily and weekly-paid workers keep their 7-day settlement cycles.
- New database table: salary_advance.
- Existing payment table gains advance_deduction.
- No existing records are deleted.
