STANCOFF VERSION 7.5.5

MONTHLY PAYMENT PERIOD CARDS
- Each worker now shows three rolling monthly cards:
  1. Previous two personal monthly cycles
  2. Current personal monthly cycle
- Cards are grouped by employee and scroll horizontally.
- The cycle dates continue to follow each employee's own first-attendance date.
- A completed payment for the exact period marks the card Paid.
- A completed previous period with no payment is clearly marked Unpaid.
- The current period is marked Current.
- Every unpaid card has a button that opens Workforce Payments with the correct
  employee, period start and period end already selected.
- Paid cards link to the payment receipt.

This makes it easier to notice an older unpaid month even after a new month begins.

DATABASE SAFETY
- No database tables or PostgreSQL records are changed.
