STANCOFF VERSION 8.8 — MHS FAQ + FLEXIBLE POST-HULLING COMBINING

- Added FAQ as a Mothers Harvest purchase type.
- FAQ means coffee already hulled but not yet processed further.
- Purchased FAQ enters Ready for Analysis and can continue into downstream production.
- Hulling parchment now creates FAQ as the authoritative hulled output/inventory product.
- Parchment may only be combined with parchment.
- Parchment cannot be combined with FAQ or any other post-hulling state.
- Non-parchment/post-hulling coffee states may be combined for suitable downstream processing.
- Hulling Only is blocked for non-parchment inputs.
- Combined post-hulling loads retain each original source lot/state through MHSProductionLot traceability.
- Full Production and optional Blacks/Triage repass behavior from 8.7 is retained.
- No database reset required. No destructive schema change.
