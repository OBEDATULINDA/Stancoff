STANCOFF UPDATE 6.8.5 - STATION TRANSFER RECEIVING

What changed
- New station transfers are saved as In Transit.
- The sending station inventory is reduced at dispatch.
- The receiving station clicks Receive and enters actual received weight, moisture and bags for each batch line.
- The app calculates received weight minus dispatched weight for every line and for the whole transfer.
- Only actual received weight is added to the receiving station inventory.
- One printable form shows dispatched, received and difference figures.

Data safety
- Existing transfer records remain unchanged.
- Existing PostgreSQL tables and data are not deleted.
- New columns are added automatically when the app starts.

Installation
1. Upload all files in Stancoff-main to the existing GitHub repository.
2. Replace matching files.
3. Commit and let Render deploy.
4. Do not delete PostgreSQL or change DATABASE_URL.
