VERSION 8.5.1 — MHS ANALYSIS PURCHASE VOLUME

Built directly on Version 8.5.

NEW ANALYSIS INFORMATION
- The MHS Analysis form now shows the total purchased weight represented by the selected batch.
- The value is pulled automatically from active MHS purchases linked to that lot/batch.
- It is read-only; the quality user does not type it manually.
- The Analysis records table now shows Purchase Volume.
- The Edit Analysis screen shows Purchase Volume.
- The printable Analysis Form shows Purchase Volume / Volume Represented.

IMPORTANT
- Sample Size remains the laboratory sample in grams.
- Purchase Volume is the commercial batch weight in kilograms represented by that analysis.
- This does not change General Outturn, Net Outturn, defects, or production calculations.
- No database schema change and no database reset.
- Because it is calculated from linked purchases, a corrected purchase weight will also be reflected on the analysis display/report.

SAFETY
- MHS analysis deletion behavior is unchanged from Version 8.5 / 8.4.3.
- Protected Stancoff routes verified unchanged:
  purchases, processing, drying, finish_drying, inventory, sales, dispatch.
